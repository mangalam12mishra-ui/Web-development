Coffee is a popular brewed beverage prepared from the roasted seeds (coffee beans) of the Coffea plant. It contains caffeine, a natural stimulant that increases alertness and energy levels.Common Types of Coffee BeansArabica: Smooth, sweet flavor with notes of fruit and berries.Robusta: Strong, bitter taste with higher caffeine content and crema.Popular Brewing MethodsDrip Coffee: Hot water filters through coffee grounds into a pot.Espresso: Forced high-pressure hot water shoots through finely-ground coffee.French Press: Coarse grounds steep directly in boiling water before pressing.Cold Brew: Coarse grounds steep in cold water for 12–24 hours.
