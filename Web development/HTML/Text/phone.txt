A mobile phone is a portable wireless device used for communication, internet browsing, and running apps. The "About Phone" section in your device settings displays key specifications, including your model number, software version, and IMEI.
Key Device InformationModel Number: Identifies your exact device make and variant.Software Version: Shows your current operating system (such as Android or iOS) and security patch level.Phone Identity: Lists hardware numbers like the IMEI and serial number.Status: Displays mobile network details, battery level, and signal strength.
