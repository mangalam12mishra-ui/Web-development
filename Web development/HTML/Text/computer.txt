A computer is an electronic machine that takes data, processes it, and gives results. It uses physical parts like a CPU and screen, and digital instructions called software, to do tasks fast.Main PartsHardware: The physical pieces you can touch, like the keyboard, mouse, screen, and CPU.Software: The programs and apps that tell the machine what to do.CPU: The central processing unit, often called the brain of the machine.Common TypesDesktop: A fixed setup with a separate tower, screen, and keyboard for home or office use.Laptop: A portable, all-in-one unit with a built-in screen and keyboard that you can carry easily.Server: A powerful system used to store and share data over a network with other devices.
